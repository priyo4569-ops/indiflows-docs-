**IndiFlows** is a fast, accurate AI voice dictation and real-time translation system designed specifically for speakers of 22+ Indian regional languages, Banglish, Hinglish, and English.

Whether you are writing emails on your Windows PC or messaging on your Android phone, IndiFlows allows you to speak naturally in your mother tongue and have your words transcribed, punctuated, and formatted into text in real time.

## Getting Started in 3 Simple Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install for Your Device">
    - **Windows:** Download and run the setup file to install the desktop app. It runs quietly in your Windows system tray.
    - **Android:** Download the APK, install it, and enable **IndiFlows Voice Keyboard** in your phone's keyboard settings.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Trigger Voice Typing">
    - **On Windows:** Place your cursor in any text field and tap the **`AltGr` (Right Alt)** key. The floating soundwave capsule appears.
    - **On Android:** Open any chat or document, and tap the **Studio Mic** button on the right side of the keyboard toolbar.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Speak and Watch It Type">
    Speak naturally at your normal speed. Tap `AltGr` again (or the checkmark on mobile). Your speech is instantly transcribed with automatic punctuation and capitalization!
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Quick Tips for Best Results

<Tip>
- **Speak at a normal conversational pace:** You don't need to speak unnaturally slow. The AI understands natural speech cadence and colloquial phrasing.
- **Punctuation is automatic:** IndiFlows automatically detects pauses and sentence endings to insert full stops (`.`, `।`) and commas. You can also say *"Full stop"* or *"New line"* explicitly.
- **Every account includes 2,000 free words per month:** Check your live quota counter in the app dashboard anytime.
</Tip>
